    How to buy Oracle packaging

    How do I order Oracle food packaging?

    Oracle packaging is supplied through our distributor network of 300+ channel partners across major cities in India. Share your product requirement and location through our enquiry form, and our team will connect you with the distributor for your region.

    Do you sell directly to restaurants, bakeries and cloud kitchens?

    Oracle supplies its packaging through our distributor network. Our distributors handle local pricing, quantities and delivery based on your requirements.

    How do I find my nearest Oracle distributor?

    Share your city and product requirement through our enquiry form or on WhatsApp. Our team will connect you with the distributor covering your region.

    Can I get samples before placing an order?

    Yes. Samples can be arranged through the distributor serving your region, depending on the order quantity you plan to buy.

    Can I buy Oracle packaging in small quantities?

    Yes. Oracle packaging is available in smaller quantities through leading packaging retailers in major cities. For bulk and regular orders, our distributors can supply you directly. Get in touch and we’ll point you to the nearest retailer or distributor.

    Where can I get the Oracle price list?

    Pricing is shared through our distributor network and depends on the product, quantity and location. Send us your requirement, and our team will connect you with the distributor for your area.

    Products, materials and specifications

    What food packaging does Oracle manufacture?

    Oracle manufactures PET containers, hinged boxes, bakery packaging, fruit punnets, sauce containers, PP containers, meal boxes, trays, lids, printed cups and biodegradable cornstarch packaging.

    What food can Oracle packaging be used for?

    Bakery products, sweets, desserts, takeaway meals, curries, sauces, fruits, salads, beverages and other foodservice and retail products.

    Are Oracle containers microwave safe, freezer safe and leak-proof?

    Our PP and cornstarch products are microwave safe and freezer safe, and our containers are leak-proof with their matching lids. PET is made for cold and room-temperature food and should not be microwaved.

    Can Oracle develop a custom size or product?

    Yes. If our existing range doesn’t fit your product, we can develop a custom size or product. Custom development involves a one-time mould cost, and the minimum order quantity is 1,00,000 pieces. Share your requirement and our team will take you through the next steps.

    Discuss a custom product →

    Can Oracle packaging carry my branding?

    Yes. We print PET and PP cups from 150 ml to 700 ml with your logo and artwork. Share your design and quantity, and our team will take you through the next steps.

    Which materials does Oracle use?

    PET for clear, cold and room-temperature food; PP for hot food, reheating and delivery; and biodegradable cornstarch for everyday foodservice.

    Quality and compliance

    Is Oracle packaging food grade?

    Yes. Oracle products are made from food-grade materials, with quality checks at every stage of manufacturing. Our products have food migration test reports, available on request for the product you need.

    What certifications does Oracle hold?

    Oracle has BRCGS Grade A certified manufacturing, to the BRCGS Global Standard for Packaging Materials (Issue 7), certified by SGS United Kingdom Ltd, a UKAS-accredited certification body. We also hold HACCP certification. The site and scope of each certificate, exactly as issued, are on our Quality & Certifications page.

    Are Oracle products BPA free?

    Yes. Oracle products are BPA free across the range.

    Distributors and export

    Does Oracle export food packaging?

    Yes. Oracle supplies food packaging to customers in 20+ international markets across the Middle East, the Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific and Africa. Visit our Export page or send us your product, destination country and quantity.

    Manufacturing

    Where is Oracle packaging manufactured?

    Across six manufacturing facilities in Silvassa, India, with 24,000 tonnes of annual production capacity, and machinery sourced from India, Japan, China and Europe.

    Is Oracle a manufacturer or a trader?

    Oracle is a manufacturer. Oracle Group has been making food packaging in India since 2000, with six manufacturing facilities in Silvassa and 400+ products in PET, PP and biodegradable Cornstarch for food businesses in India and export markets.

    Who does Oracle supply food packaging to?

    Bakeries and sweet shops; hotels, restaurants and cafés; QSRs and cloud kitchens; caterers and event services; fruit and fresh produce businesses; and dairy and packaged food brands.

    What is the difference between Oracle Group, Oracle Polyplast and Oracle Polypack?

    Oracle Group is the name we trade under. Oracle Polyplast and Oracle Polypack Private Limited are the two manufacturing companies in the group, running six facilities in Silvassa.

    Cornstarch and sustainable packaging

    Does Oracle offer biodegradable food packaging?

    Yes. Oracle’s Cornstarch range is made from cornstarch and is biodegradable, offering a more sustainable alternative to conventional plastic food packaging.

    What is cornstarch packaging and what can it be used for?

    Cornstarch packaging is made using cornstarch, a renewable plant-based material that is moulded into food packaging. Oracle’s range includes meal boxes, trays, plates, bowls and containers for meals, curries, snacks, desserts and condiments.

    Is cornstarch packaging suitable for hot food, microwave and freezer use?

    Yes. Oracle’s Cornstarch range is suitable for hot and cold food and is microwave safe and freezer safe.

    Is cornstarch packaging strong enough for everyday use?

    Yes. It’s strong and sturdy, resists leaks, oil, grease and impact, and holds its shape during handling.

    How does Oracle approach sustainability?

    Through three things: recycled PET (rPET) in selected products, a biodegradable cornstarch range, and 1,600 kW of solar power that meets close to 30% of our factories’ connected electricity load.

    Do you offer rPET packaging?

    Yes. Our punnets are made from rPET (recycled PET), along with selected bakery containers.
