04 — Materials

The right material for every food.

Five material families, each chosen for how the food behaves — clarity for produce, recycled content for circular packaging, heat for meals, rigidity for bakery, and biodegradability where it matters.

PET

Crystal clear

High clarity and stiffness for shelf appeal. Widely recyclable.

Used for

  • Fruit & berry punnets
  • Salad clamshells
  • Deli & sweet trays

Shown: 1625 Strawberry punnet, 200 g

rPET

Recycled & crystal clear

Made with recycled PET content. Keeps the clarity and strength of PET while reducing the need for new plastic.

Used for

  • Fruit & berry punnets
  • Retail & export packs
  • Brands with recycled-content targets

Shown: 1648 Berry punnet SQ, 125 g

PP

Heat tolerant

Handles hot fill and reheating. Tough and chemical-resistant.

Used for

  • Ready meals
  • Takeaway containers
  • Sweets & namkeen trays

Shown: PP rectangular containers

HIPS

Rigid & economical

Opaque, easy to form and cost-efficient.

Used for

  • Cake & pastry bases
  • Bakery inserts
  • Dry goods trays

Shown: 1757 Dry cake box 9″, black with lid

CORNSTARCH

Biodegradable

Plant-based material for brands with sustainability targets and lower-impact packaging goals.

Used for

  • Compartment meal boxes
  • Food service
  • Eco-conscious brands

Shown: 5606C 5-compartment meal box
