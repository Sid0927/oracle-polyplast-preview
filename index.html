Materials

Which material is right for your food?

PET for clear, cold and room-temperature food; PP for hot food, reheating and delivery; and cornstarch for biodegradable foodservice.

PET

Clear, for cold and room-temperature food

Crystal clear and rigid, so food looks its best on the shelf. Designed for cold and room-temperature food, not for the microwave.

Good for

  • Fruit & berry punnets
  • Salads, desserts & sweets
  • Bakery & dry foods

PP

Built for hot food and delivery

Handles hot food, reheating and delivery. Microwave safe and freezer safe.

Good for

  • Meal boxes & thalis
  • Takeaway & delivery
  • Curries & hot meals

CORNSTARCH

Biodegradable, made for everyday foodservice

Made from cornstarch. Strong and sturdy, leak-proof, microwave safe and freezer safe.

Good for

  • Meal boxes & trays
  • Plates & bowls
  • Cutlery

How do I order Oracle food packaging?

Oracle packaging is supplied through our distributor network of 300+ channel partners across major cities in India. Share your product requirement and location through our enquiry form, and our team will connect you with the distributor for your region.

Can I get samples before placing an order?

Yes. Samples can be arranged through the distributor serving your region, depending on the order quantity you plan to buy.

Are Oracle containers microwave safe, freezer safe and leak-proof?

Our PP and cornstarch products are microwave safe and freezer safe, and our containers are leak-proof with their matching lids. PET is made for cold and room-temperature food and should not be microwaved.

Can Oracle develop a custom size or format?

Yes. If our existing range doesn’t fit your product, we can develop a custom size or format. Custom development involves a one-time mould cost, and the minimum order quantity is 1,00,000 pieces. Share your requirement and our team will take you through the next steps.
