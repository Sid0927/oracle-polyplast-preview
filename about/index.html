About

Oracle Group is a food packaging manufacturer in India.

Oracle Group is a food packaging manufacturer in India, comprising Oracle Polyplast and Oracle Polypack Private Limited, with six manufacturing facilities in Silvassa.

Explore products Request a quote

Vision

To be the most trusted name in food packaging for every business that serves food in India and beyond.

Mission

We make food packaging that works hard, so food businesses can focus on their food, not their packaging.

Our story

Built on consistency. Growing through trust.

In 2000, Rajesh Gandhi and Deepak Agarwal saw a gap in India's food packaging industry. Businesses needed packaging that was professionally manufactured, consistent in quality and built to be depended on.

We started with HIPS water cups. More than 25 years later, Oracle has grown across multiple manufacturing facilities and a range of 400+ products, while the principle behind the business remains the same:

Make packaging that businesses can rely on.

Milestones

25+ years of building Oracle

  1. 2000 Oracle founded
  2. 2009 First export order and entry into international markets
  3. 2014 Rakholi, Silvassa Unit 1

  8. 2025 Sayli, Silvassa Unit 4

Our plants

Six manufacturing facilities in Silvassa.

Across Oracle Polyplast and Oracle Polypack, with around 24,000 tonnes of production capacity a year: four units in Rakholi and two in Sayli.

Know us

Trusted at home. Chosen abroad.
