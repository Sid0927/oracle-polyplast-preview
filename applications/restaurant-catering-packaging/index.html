Do you have biodegradable options for events?

Yes. Our Cornstarch range covers bowls, containers, cutlery, meal trays, plates and trays. It is microwave safe and freezer safe.

Can these trays be reheated?

PP and Cornstarch meal trays are microwave safe. PET should not be microwaved.
