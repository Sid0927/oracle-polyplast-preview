Which material should I use for hot food?

PP. It handles hot food, reheating and delivery, and is microwave safe and freezer safe. PET is for cold and room-temperature food.

Do your meal boxes come with lids?

Yes. Each PP meal box has a matching lid, listed beside the box on its product page.

How quickly can you dispatch?

In-stock items dispatch from our factory within 48 hours. Tell us the products and quantities and we will confirm.
