What BRCGS certification means for packaging buyers

By [Author name] Published [date] Last updated [date]

BRCGS publishes the Global Standard for Packaging Materials, a food-safety standard for companies that make packaging. A certificate shows that a site was audited against it by an accredited certification body.

A certificate covers a site and a scope

Each certificate names the site that was audited and the products and processes in scope. Oracle’s BRCGS certificate covers sheet extrusion and thermoforming of cups, lids, plates, trays, punnets, hinged boxes, containers and sheets for direct and indirect food contact. Our Quality & Certifications page shows the site and scope of each certificate exactly as issued.

Reading the grade

The audit grade reflects how many issues the auditor found. Oracle’s certificate is Grade A, from an announced audit by SGS United Kingdom Ltd, a UKAS-accredited certification body.

Check it yourself

Certificates are listed in the BRCGS Directory. Look up the site and check the certificate number, scope and expiry date before you rely on it. Our Quality & Certifications page lists the details as issued.

FAQs

What certifications does Oracle hold?

Oracle has BRCGS Grade A certified manufacturing, to the BRCGS Global Standard for Packaging Materials (Issue 7), certified by SGS United Kingdom Ltd, a UKAS-accredited certification body. We also hold HACCP certification. The site and scope of each certificate, exactly as issued, are on our Quality & Certifications page.

Is Oracle packaging food grade?

Yes. Oracle products are made from food-grade materials, with quality checks at every stage of manufacturing. Our products have food migration test reports, available on request for the product you need.

Can I get a migration test report?

Yes. Ask us for the report for the product you need.
