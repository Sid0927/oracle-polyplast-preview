PET vs PP: which is right for hot food?

By [Author name] Published [date] Last updated [date]

If you pack hot food, the material matters as much as the shape. PET and PP look similar on the shelf, but they behave very differently once heat is involved.

PET: clear, for cold and room-temperature food

PET is crystal clear and rigid, so food looks its best. It is designed for cold and room-temperature food such as fruit, salads, desserts, sweets and bakery, and should not be microwaved.

PP: made for hot food, reheating and delivery

PP handles hot food. Oracle PP containers and meal boxes are microwave safe and freezer safe, which makes them the usual choice for curries, rice, thalis and delivery.

A quick rule

Hot, reheated or delivered: choose PP. Cold, displayed or room temperature: choose PET. For a biodegradable option for hot and cold food, look at our Cornstarch range.

FAQs

Can I microwave PET containers?

No. PET is designed for cold and room-temperature food and should not be microwaved.

Are Oracle PP containers freezer safe?

Yes. Our PP products are microwave safe and freezer safe.

Is there a biodegradable option for hot food?

Yes. Oracle’s Cornstarch range is suitable for hot and cold food and is microwave safe and freezer safe.
