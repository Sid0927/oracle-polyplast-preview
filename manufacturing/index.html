Our process

From material to finished pack.

Five steps, from sheet to dispatch.

Sheet extrusion

The same material quality in every order, because we never depend on an outside sheet supplier.

Highlights

  • In-house sheet extrusion
  • Sheet quality controlled from the start
  • PET, PP and Cornstarch sheet

Thermoforming

Even walls, even on deep containers, so corners don't crack and lids sit right.

Highlights

  • Plug-assist forming for even walls
  • Hygiene by design: enclosed forming area

Automated production

Less handling, so your packaging arrives cleaner and more consistent.

Highlights

  • Fully automatic lines
  • Forming, trimming, counting, stacking and packaging in one line
  • Minimal human contact

Quality checks

Problems are caught at our plant, not at your counter.

Highlights

  • Food-grade materials
  • Food migration test reports
  • BRCGS Grade A certified manufacturing

Packing & dispatch

Repeat orders move fast, because most of the range is already in stock.

Highlights

  • 80% of the range kept in stock
  • In-stock items dispatch within 48 hours
  • Export documentation support
