Our process

From material to finished pack.

Six steps, from sheet to dispatch.

Sheet extrusion

Consistent sheet production.

We extrude our own sheet in-house, so quality is controlled from the very first step.

Highlights

  • In-house sheet extrusion
  • Sheet quality controlled from the start
  • PET, PP and Cornstarch sheet

Thermoforming

Precise forming across multiple formats.

Hytec plug-assist forming gives even wall thickness, even on deep draws.

Highlights

  • Precision forming: Hytec plug-assist
  • Even wall thickness on deep draws
  • Hygiene by design: enclosed forming area
[Photo — In-house tooling]

In-house tooling

Faster development and maintenance.

Moulds are maintained, repaired and adjusted at our own plant.

Highlights

  • Moulds maintained in-house
  • Repairs and adjustments on site
  • Custom formats developed in-house

Automated production

Forming, trimming, counting and stacking.

Fully automatic lines take each product from forming to packing in one line, with minimal human contact.

Highlights

  • Fully automatic lines
  • Forming, trimming, counting, stacking and packaging in one line
  • Minimal human contact

Quality checks

Defined checks through production.

Products are made from food-grade materials, with checks through production and food migration test reports.

Highlights

  • Food-grade materials
  • Food migration test reports
  • BRCGS Grade A at our Rakholi site

Packing & dispatch

Prepared for repeat supply.

80% of the range is kept in stock, so repeat orders move quickly.

Highlights

  • 80% of the range kept in stock
  • In-stock items dispatch within 48 hours
  • Export documentation support
