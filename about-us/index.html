About us

A manufacturer and exporter of plastic food packaging, supplying food businesses in India and around the world since 2000.

Vision

Where there is food, there is Oracle.

Our story

Built on consistency. Growing through trust.

In 2000, Rajesh Gandhi and Deepak Agarwal saw a gap in India's food packaging industry. Businesses needed packaging that was professionally manufactured, consistent in quality and built to be depended on.

[Photo — founders / early plant]

We started with HIPS water cups. More than 25 years later, Oracle has grown across multiple manufacturing facilities and a range of 450 products and more, while the principle behind the business remains the same:

Make packaging that businesses can rely on.
