About Us

A manufacturer and exporter of plastic food packaging, supplying food businesses in India and around the world since 2000.

VISION

Where there is food, there is Oracle. As an innovative, dynamic and reliable manufacturer of plastic food packaging products, our vision is to stand out as an ideal performer in national and international markets.

MISSION

We create and deliver improved products and services that meet the changing requirements and expectations of our channel partners across the globe, with uncompromising integrity and excellence in everything that carries the Oracle name.

KNOW US

Founded in 2000, Oracle Group is a recognized manufacturer and exporter of plastic food packaging products. A network of around 300 channel partners supports customers across all the major cities of the country.

Our products are well established with domestic and overseas customers alike. Built on a strong reputation and many years of cooperation, Oracle Group is a trusted supplier of plastic food packaging products to buyers in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Fiji, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UAE, Kuwait, Singapore, Cyprus, New Zealand, Iraq, Colombia, Mauritius, Aruba, Thailand and Madagascar.

Built on consistency. Growing through trust.

In 2000, Rajesh Gandhi and Deepak Agarwal saw a gap in India's food packaging industry. Businesses needed packaging that was professionally manufactured, consistent in quality and built to be depended on.

We started with HIPS water cups. More than 25 years later, Oracle has grown across multiple manufacturing facilities and a range of 450 products and more, while the principle behind the business remains the same:

Make packaging that businesses can rely on.
